## Protagonists

**Lucia Caminos** is the first female lead of a mainline GTA. She grew up in Liberty City, where her father taught her to fight. She was jailed in Leonida Penitentiary for a crime tied to protecting her family. After release she works with a correctional social worker (Stefanie).

**Jason Duval** is an Army veteran who grew up around grifters. He runs drugs and shakedowns in the Leonida Keys for Brian Heder, with his friend Cal Hampton, and lives in one of Brian's properties.

The pair are written as a Bonnie-and-Clyde couple. You play both and can switch between them, including when they are in different places.

### Map size (gtadb FAQ)

- Land area: about 82.7 km², 1.66x GTA V
- All modelled terrain including sea floor: about 187 km², 2.23x GTA V
- Highest points: Mount Kalaga and the Bluegrass transmission tower, both about 404 m (Mount Chiliad in GTA V is 803 m)
- Game units are roughly metres. On the gtadb map, game (0,0) is the map centre, X runs east, Y runs north, and the tile grid covers -16384..16384 on both axes.

## Gameplay systems shown so far

- **Character switching** between Jason and Lucia, including mid-activity
- **Wanted level up to six stars.** Police identify you by clothes, looks, vehicle, and whether you are together. Changing cars, outfits and disguises shakes them.
- **Criminal Profile**, one per protagonist. It tracks how you commit crimes, e.g. unnecessary violence, rather than how many.
- **Relationship System** between Jason and Lucia (holding hands, doing activities together)
- Body changes: weight gain and loss, visible sleep deprivation
- Customisation: hair, clothes, accessories, tattoos
- Activities: gym and weightlifting, basketball, scuba, kayaking, BASE jumping, skydiving, wrestling, jet-skis, dirt bikes, strip clubs, golf (golf clubs are in the weapon list), store robberies
- Social media parody: in-game feeds, Snapmatic, "Leonida Man" culture
- NPCs can be greeted or taunted. Petting a dog registers on the Criminal Profile.
